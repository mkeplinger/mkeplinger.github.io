Privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-07.

Personal Brain is used by one person, its owner, on their own Google account.

What it accesses

Read-only access to the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Tasks, and permission to send one email a day from the owner's account to the owner's own address.

How the data is used

Only to compose that daily summary. Data stays on the owner's computer and in the owner's own mailbox. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train models. No data from any other person's account is accessed.

Google API Services

Personal Brain's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
